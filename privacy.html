Molly privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What Molly is

Molly is a private personal-assistant app that runs on the user's own Mac. It is used only by members of one household and is not offered to the public.

Google data

When you connect your Google account, Molly can read, label and draft emails in your Gmail, and read and add events in your Google Calendar. It does this only to show you your own email and calendar and to carry out actions you ask for. Molly sends an email only when you explicitly tell it to.

Your Google data and sign-in tokens are stored only on your own Mac. They are not sent to the app's author or to any server run by the app's author, and they are not sold or shared for advertising.

When you ask Molly a question, the relevant text may be sent to Claude (Anthropic) through your own Claude account so it can help you. That use is covered by your agreement with Anthropic.

Molly's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google in Molly's settings at any time, or remove Molly's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Molly's folder in your Mac's Library removes all data it stored.

Contact

Use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Molly.
